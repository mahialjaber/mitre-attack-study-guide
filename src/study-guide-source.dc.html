01 · The vocabulary

Four words, in strict hierarchy

Get this hierarchy wrong and every conversation about ATT&CK goes sideways. Tactic answers why, technique answers how, procedure answers exactly how, by whom.

TACTIC · TA0002
The why

The adversary's tactical goal — the reason for an action. There are only 15, which is why they are the memorisation priority.

Execution
TECHNIQUE · T1059
The how

The means of achieving the goal. One technique can serve several tactics — that many-to-many mapping is deliberate.

Command and Scripting Interpreter
SUB-TECHNIQUE · T1059.001
The specific how

A more precise description of the same behaviour. This is the level detections should usually be written and reported at.

PowerShell
PROCEDURE · no ID
The observed instance

A specific in-the-wild implementation by a named group or tool. Procedures are the evidence the whole framework is built from.

Matrices & platforms

Tactics are shared conceptually, but each matrix has its own technique set. Never quote an Enterprise technique ID for an ICS behaviour.

Enterprise · the default
Windows · macOS · Linux · Office Suite · Identity Provider · SaaS · IaaS · Network Devices · Containers · ESXi · PRE
Mobile
Android · iOS. Adds tactics absent from Enterprise, e.g. Network Effects and Remote Service Effects.
ICS / OT
Control systems. Distinct tactics: Inhibit Response Function, Impair Process Control. Safety, not confidentiality, is the crown jewel.
PRE — the pre-attack half
Reconnaissance and Resource Development happen outside your estate. You will almost never have telemetry for them; treat them as intel and attack-surface problems, not detection gaps.

05 · What changed in v19

Defense Evasion is gone. Two tactics took its place.

Defense Evasion was always a mix of behaviour and intent: “renaming a binary” and “killing the EDR agent” sat in the same column despite demanding completely different responses. v19 scopes them to adversary intent.

Stealth TA0005 · inherited

Making malicious activity indistinguishable from legitimate behaviour. The defining characteristic: your defences are still fully operational — the adversary simply looks normal to them.

T1036 Masquerading
T1027 Obfuscated Files or Information
T1218 System Binary Proxy Execution
T1564 Hide Artifacts
T1070 Indicator Removal
SOC response: analytic. Hunt, correlate, baseline. A single Stealth signal is usually low-confidence on its own.
Defense Impairment TA0112 · NEW ID

Actively disabling, degrading or compromising the security controls themselves. Not hiding from your defences — breaking them.

T1685 Disable or Modify Tools
T1686 Disable or Modify System Firewall
T1687 Exploitation for Defense Impairment
T1490 Inhibit System Recovery
T1489 Service Stop
SOC response: contain now. Tamper attempts are one of the strongest indicators of a hands-on-keyboard adversary — escalate and validate control integrity.

Coverage heat-mapping with Navigator

ATT&CK Navigator renders JSON layers over the matrix. The standard analyst play is three layers, then look at the overlap:

Threat layer — techniques used by groups that target your sector
Visibility layer — what your telemetry could theoretically see
Detection layer — what you have a tuned, tested rule for
Red = threat, no detection. That intersection is your roadmap. Never report raw technique count as “coverage”.

Threat-informed defense — the loop the framework exists to enable

CTI describes what adversaries do in ATT&CK terms → detection engineering builds analytics for it → the red team emulates it → the results correct the map. Break any link and ATT&CK degrades into a compliance sticker.
